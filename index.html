    Hello, my name is Ben Beary, and I am an indie developer. I have a BFA in Digital Media Arts from San Jose State University. My journey started in high school, where I learned coding languages like JavaScript and C#, alongside art programs such as Photoshop, Illustrator, and Blender. In college, I continued to expand my knowledge of Game Design, honing my skills in gameplay programming, UI/UX design, world building, and creating polished player experiences.

    I have experience in designing levels, scripting mechanics, creating audio, 3D modeling, and managing full production pipelines for multi-scene projects. I enjoy solving problems with modular, efficient code and building tools that help improve workflows.
